Get ready for a fresh take on puzzle gaming with "Whats the Password?" launching on May 28, 2026. This minimalistic game from solo developer Dan DiIorio of TrampolineTales focuses on decoding 4-digit passwords, offering over 100 puzzles designed to evoke that satisfying "aha" moment. With a clean black and white aesthetic, the game promises to deliver pure puzzle-solving without the distractions of complex RPG systems or crafting mechanics.

Available on Android, iOS, and PC, the game will be priced at $4.99 on the Google Play Store. It supports 18 languages, making it accessible to a global audience. The music, composed by the talent behind "Luck be a Landlord," sets the perfect tone for an engaging puzzle experience. Are you ready to challenge your mind and decode some passwords?

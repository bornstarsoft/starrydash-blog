The launch of Phase 2 for "Neverness to Everness" marks a significant moment in the gaming community, especially as it unfolds against the backdrop of ongoing AI controversies. Despite the challenges, Hotta Studio is pushing forward with exciting updates, including a new limited S-class character, Hotori, and a fresh arc banner, all of which are set to enhance the gameplay experience until June 3.

The introduction of the Misty Tipsy Style Limited Board not only features Hotori but also incorporates a player-friendly gacha mechanic that allows pity rolls to carry over to future banners. This approach is a refreshing change in the gacha landscape, which often frustrates players with reset mechanics. As players engage with the new content, the community will be watching closely how the developers navigate the ongoing discussions surrounding AI-generated assets in gaming.

As we look ahead, the anticipation builds for the upcoming characters Lacrimosa and Chaos, who promise to bring even more depth to the game. Will these new additions help to shift the narrative away from the controversy, or will they deepen the conversation around AI in gaming?
